2. Global fill caps [170,340,45] exhaust edge-first, not deficit-first.

   (needs_dense_access ≥10000, alley roll decides) → max_courtyard 0.
5. Invalid-terrain empties <4000 m² stay "unbuilt" instead of designed parks.
