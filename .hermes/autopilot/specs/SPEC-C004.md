## Player-facing objective

Outside the 350 m historic basin and beyond the river valley, the world must read as inhabited Czech countryside rather than empty hills with scattered trees. From the plaza the player can follow a readable rural network: city gates where paved roads leave the basin, secondary village roads branching toward deterministic settlement anchors (villages, hamlets, isolated farmsteads) scored for slope, flood risk, water access, soil and spacing, and farm tracks along field edges — with river crossings only where hydrology already exposes a ford/bridge corridor. Road surfaces are terrain-conforming vertex-colored ribbons with narrow bridge decks where a road must cross water, streamed beside city+terrain+water+biome with measured ACTIVE-only budgets. The network gives the player landmarks, destinations, route choice (road vs cross-country), and a crossing puzzle at the Vltava-like river that the next building/industry layers will populate.

This is the next bounded milestone because the geographic logic stack is now landed and streamed: terrain 17x17 289/512 1 collider 9 active, hydrology Vltava-like CX 620+-90 meander 72+18 width 38-50 9x9 81/128 9 active, rural mosaic biomes/geology 9x9 81/128 `<=48` instances 9 active biome — each proved deterministic, seam-continuous within `0.02` and ACTIVE-only. The hinterland is visually distinct (floodplain/wet meadow/field/forest/quarry) but still has no places or paths: without settlement anchors every bearing is equivalent, without a road graph traversal has no reason, and future village buildings, farms, quarries, rail and vertical networks would be placed arbitrarily. Settlement suitability and its road graph are the macro plan's "geographic logic precedes decoration" gate for Phase 4 — they must be established before any building program, industry corridor, or upper-city retrofit.

This slice is deliberately thin and additive: no village building footprints or interiors, no rail mesh, no lake/reservoir basin formation, no terrain trench carve through the historic core, no underground, no swimming, no authored modular road or building assets, no toon shader rework. It adds deterministic settlement scoring as pure plan data plus a hierarchical road graph whose edges are materialized as low-cost per-chunk ribbons/bridge decks streamed with the same budget discipline as terrain/water/biome.

## Gameplay value

Improves at least four enjoyment pillars in one thin slice while preserving budgets:

- **Exploration and discovery** — deterministic village/hamlet/farmstead anchors (spaced on macro-cell, not per-chunk speckles) become readable destinations at kilometer scale; a player can orient by "the village north of the ford" and return by the same road.
- **Agency and meaningful choice** — road hierarchy (primary paved `~7 m`, secondary gravel `~5 m`, farm track `~3.5 m`) gives a movement-cost vs exposure trade-off (faster on road, visible to hazards; slower cross-country through fields/forest). The river is now a gated crossing: roads meet water only at `crossing_candidates` with a bridge/ford deck, so the player must seek a crossing rather than wade anywhere.
- **Coherent Czech identity** — Bohemian hamlet spacing, farm tracks along field/hedgerow parcels, floodplain-avoiding village sits and bridge placements align with the Vltava-like valley already in the world.
- **Performance and stability foundation** — settlements are pure plan anchors (no building meshes yet), roads reuse the measured streaming discipline: deterministic manifests, world-space shared edges/centerlines within `SEAM_CONTINUITY_TOL_M`, bounded verts/tris/colliders/instances, ACTIVE-only physics, `t_road_gen`/`t_road_mat` telemetry within `FRAME_BUDGET_MS 12`, save exclusion of generated road geometry, `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 2 (additive).

Cosmetic polish accompanies but does not replace the playable system: road surfaces are flat vertex-colored ribbons plus a single bridge deck Box where a road must span water; no imported road textures, no rail, no village buildings.

## Scope

### 1. Deterministic settlement plan

Create `world/generation/settlement_plan.gd` as a pure plan (no Node access, no unseeded randomness, no chunk-local state) and expose it through `world/generation/world_plan.gd` facade.

Required queries (exact names may be adjusted but semantics must exist and be used by the road graph and future building layers):

- `settlement_anchors() -> Array[Dictionary]` — all anchors deterministically scattered over the `[-8192,8192)` world. Each dict: `{id: String, kind: StringName, center: Vector2, radius: float, bounds: Rect2, suitability: float, dist_to_water: float, slope_deg: float, strata: StringName, tile: Vector2i}`. `kind` vocab subset of `WorldConstants.SETTLEMENT_VOCAB` (at least `&"village"`, `&"hamlet"`, `&"farmstead"`, `&"isolated_farm"`; `&"town"` may appear sparingly on arterial intersections but never inside `URBAN_INNER_M`). `radius` is `48-90` for village, `26-46` for hamlet, `16-28` for farmstead, deterministic from seeded hash.
- `settlements_in(rect: Rect2) -> Array[Dictionary]` — anchors whose `bounds` intersect the rect or whose `center` lies inside `rect.grow(180)`.
- `nearest_settlement(p: Vector2) -> Dictionary` — closest anchor to `p` (empty dict if world empty, which never happens for canonical seeds).
- `settlement_at(p: Vector2) -> Dictionary` — anchor whose `bounds` contains `p` or whose `center` distance `< radius*0.75` and not separated by water (uses `hydrology.distance_to_water` gate); otherwise empty.
- `is_settlement_center(p: Vector2, kind_filter: StringName = &"") -> bool` — convenience predicate.
- `city_gates() -> Array[Vector2]` — `4-8` deterministic gate positions on the ring `URBAN_OUTER_M +- 60` (and optionally an inner ring at `URBAN_INNER_M+80`) where city avenues meet rural roads. Gates are the graph roots.

Generation contract:

- Settlement scoring uses `WorldSeed.sample_coherent` / `sample_coherent_signed` / `unit_float` with explicit settlement domains and `MACRO_CELL_M 1024` / `LANDSCAPE_CELL_M 256` spacing, seed-separated from terrain/hydro/geology/biome domains. No RNG sharing, no per-query mutable state, handles negative world coordinates via `floori`.
- Per-macro-cell candidate generation: for each `1024 m` cell, place `0-2` candidates with seeded jitter inside the cell via `unit_float("settlement_jitter", [cx, cy, k])`, then score each candidate with a layered function:
  1. Base field: `score += sample_coherent(p, &"settlement_field", 520)` (region clustering already used for forest/field macro).
  2. Slope gate: `terrain.slope_at(p)` must be `< BUILDABLE_MAX_SLOPE_DEG` for hamlet/farmstead and `< 14deg` for village; `terrain_class == &"cliff"` rejects.
  3. Flood gate: `hydrology.distance_to_water(p) > BANK_W + FLOODPLAIN_W + 14` for village, `> BANK_W + 8` for hamlet, `> 2` for farmstead; `is_floodplain(p)` rejects villages, `water_body_at(p) != &""` rejects all.
  4. Soil/fertility + strata: `geology.fertility_at(p)` and `geology.strata_at(p)` bias: farmstead prefers `loess/alluvial` with `fertility > 0.48`, hamlet prefers `fertility > 0.42` or `soil == loess_soil`, `rocky_quarry` biome rejects all except an isolated quarry outpost (deferred — not placed here).
  5. Water access: `dist_to_water in [80, 520]` preferred for village (well/river access without flood risk), scored via smooth peak.
  6. Spacing inhibition: after ranking candidates by scored suitability, greedily accept in descending score while enforcing minimum center separation `village 700 m`, `hamlet 420 m`, `farmstead 220 m` and at least `1.8 * radius` to any stronger accepted anchor; this yields `12-30` total anchors in the `[-3072,3072)` playable annulus around the basin plus scattered outliers, deterministic and not per-chunk speckles.
  7. Urban exclusion: no anchor whose `center.length() < URBAN_OUTER_M + 180` and whose `kind == &"village"` is accepted unless it is a city-gate satellite hamlet within `520-880` of a gate (rare, documented). The dense `URBAN_INNER_M 350` flat never hosts a rural anchor.
- Gates: deterministic `phi_k = unit_float("settlement_gate_phi", [k]) * TAU` for `k in 0..7` filtered by a suitability test identical to hamlet scoring at the ring radius; keep `4-8` strongest; each gate stores `center`, `angle`, `radius 18`, `id "gate_<k>"`.
- Contract is pure and additive: does not mutate `TerrainPlan` heights, does not carve `CityPlan` blocks, `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 2. Determinism holds for any seed and any query order, including negative coords.

### 2. Deterministic hierarchical road network plan

Create `world/generation/road_network_plan.gd` as a pure plan that reads `TerrainPlan`, `HydrologyPlan`, `BiomePlan`, `GeologyPlan`, and `SettlementPlan` (injected by `WorldPlan`) plus its own road fields.

Required queries:

- `road_graph() -> Dictionary` — stable graph `{nodes: Array[Dictionary], edges: Array[Dictionary]}` where nodes are `center: Vector2` plus `id, kind: StringName (&"city_gate"|&"village"|&"hamlet"|&"farmstead"|&"crossing"|&"junction")` and edges are `{id: String, a: String, b: String, hierarchy: StringName, width: float, length: float, polyline: PackedVector2Array, is_bridge: bool, water_id: String, crossing_id: String}`.
- `road_segments_in(rect: Rect2) -> Array[Dictionary]` — edges clipped to rect with precomputed `polyline_clipped: PackedVector2Array` (world-space world coords, at least 2 points if crossing the rect), plus `hierarchy/width/is_bridge` and `center` (midpoint of clipped segment).
- `road_hierarchy_at(p: Vector2) -> StringName` — `&"primary"`|`&"secondary"`|`&"track"` or `&""` if off-road (distance to nearest road centerline `> width*0.6+1.2`).
- `distance_to_road(p: Vector2) -> float` — unsigned distance to nearest road centerline (INF if graph empty for that seed, which never happens canonically).
- `nearest_crossing(p: Vector2) -> Dictionary` — closest bridge/ford from `hydrology.crossing_candidates` that a road actually uses.
- ` road_width_at(p: Vector2) -> float` — nominal width at that world point (0 if off-road).

Generation contract (layered, macro-cell contiguous, hydrology-constrained):

1. Nodes: union of `settlement_plan.settlement_anchors()` plus `city_gates()` plus hydrology crossing anchors that roads elect to use. Crossing anchors are chosen from `hydrology.crossing_candidates(Rect2(world), expanded 8192)` filtered by road proximity: for each candidate `c` within `480` of any settlement or gate, keep if a road edge would need to cross its water. Each kept crossing becomes a node `kind &"crossing"` with `center = c.center`, `width = c.width`, `water_id` from hydrology.
2. Edges: deterministic Minimum Spanning Tree over all non-crossing nodes using world distance weighted by `1 + 0.25 * slope_penalty + 0.30 * water_penalty + 0.10 * forest_penalty`, where `slope_penalty = clamp((avg_slope-8)/14,0,1)` sampled every `32 m` along the straight candidate, `water_penalty` is large unless the straight segment passes through a kept crossing corridor, and `forest_penalty` biases toward existing village rims rather than deep forest interior. Build graph with seeded Kruskal (stable sort by weight then id). Then add `1-3` seeded extra edges for loops where a second shortest alternative would shorten a detour by `> 22%` (keeps the network from being a single tree while staying sparse).
3. Hierarchy assignment: any edge incident to a `city_gate` and carrying a primary settlement (`village` or gate-to-primary crossing) is `&"primary"` (`ROAD_WIDTH_PRIMARY 7.0`, paved palette); edges between `village--hamlet` or `village--village` that are not primary are `&"secondary"` (`ROAD_WIDTH_SECONDARY 5.0`, gravel); remaining `hamlet--farmstead`/`farmstead--farmstead`/`forest spur` edges are `&"track"` (`ROAD_WIDTH_TRACK 3.5`, dirt). At most one hierarchy per edge.
4. Geometry: each edge polyline is seeded-smoothed from `a` to `b` via 3-point bezier (`a -> mid -> b`) where `mid = lerp(a,b,0.5) + perpendicular * offset`, `offset = sample_coherent_signed((a+b)*0.5, &"road_mid", 300) * clamp(length*0.08, 6, 28)` plus a second `sample_coherent` nudge to avoid cliff spikes. Sampled every `10-14` m along the bezier (spec requires builder sampling every `12 m`). The polyline is then filtered to reject any subsegment whose average `terrain.slope_at` `>= CLIFF_SLOPE_DEG` or whose `hydrology.distance_to_water` would imply a water crossing outside a kept `crossing_candidates` corridor (reject and reroute via nearby crossing; if no crossing within `420` the edge itself is dropped rather than cutting water).
5. Bridges: wherever a polyline crosses a water body (`hydrology.distance_to_water < bank+3` and `hydrology.water_body_at != &""` along the crossing), the crossing subpolyline is promoted to `is_bridge=true`, `water_id = hydrology.water_body_id_at(mid)`, `crossing_id = candidate.id`. Bridge deck centerline matches road centerline; deck length equals water width plus `2*4 m` bank abutments; deck width = road width + `0.6`.
6. Pure and deterministic: every query is function of `seed + domain + world coordinates` via `WorldSeed.sample_coherent*` / `unit_float`; no RNG sharing between unrelated road/settlement queries; no chunk visit order dependence; inclusive of negative world coordinates; polyline sampling uses world-space shared edges (chunk builder will clip deterministically).

### 3. Road and bridge chunk materialization

Create `world/streaming/road_chunk_builder.gd` and integrate through `WorldPlan` + `ChunkManager`.

Manifest per 64 m chunk via `static build_manifest(world_plan: WorldPlan, coord: Vector2i) -> Dictionary`:

- Keys: `coord, origin, size, road_segments (Array[Dictionary] clipped), bridge_segments (Array[Dictionary] clipped), road_vertices, road_triangles, road_colliders, bridge_vertices, bridge_triangles, bridge_colliders, has_road, has_bridge, road_widths (Array[float]), hierarchies (Array[StringName]), colors (PackedColorArray per segment), road_gen_ms, road_mat_ms_placeholder`. Deterministic for `(seed, coord)`.
- Sampling: for the chunk rect `Rect2(origin, Vector2(64,64))` inflated by `half_width+2` to capture edge-crossers, query `road_network_plan.road_segments_in(rect)` (already clipped). For each clipped polyline with `n` points (`n >=2`), tessellate a flat ribbon: for each segment `(p0,p1)` compute left/right offset `perp * width*0.5`, generate two vertices per point at `terrain.height_at(p) + ROAD_LIFT_M (0.04-0.05)` (lift avoids z-fighting like biome `0.03` but slightly higher so road reads above field tint). Interpolate height bilinearly along the ribbon; normals are `Vector3.UP` for the flat road material. Indices are two triangles per quad `(0,1,2, 2,1,3)` in consistent winding. Bridge decks are tessellated similarly but at `water_level_at(p0) + BRIDGE_DECK_LIFT_M 0.35` above water where over water, tapering to terrain+lift at abutments; deck is a simple Box-like flat strip, not a full truss.
- Visual palette: `primary` `Color("7a7a78")` paved grey, `secondary` `Color("8b7f6e")` gravel tan, `track` `Color("6e5d4b")` dirt brown, `bridge` deck `Color("6a6a6a")` with `Color("5a4a3a")` abutment edge; vertex colors via `StandardMaterial3D vertex_color_use_as_albedo=true` (no texture import in this slice).
- Collision budget: at most **one road collider per chunk** (0 if no road). The single collider is one aggregated `ConcavePolygonShape3D` (or at most two `BoxShape3D` strips) under `StaticBody3D RoadBody` (`collision_layer 1`, `mask 0`, `backface_collision=true` for Concave). Bridge decks share the same body (deck collision is coplanar with road, no extra body). Dry/no-road chunks have 0. Do not add per-segment bodies, per-waypoint volumes, or navmesh. Warm retains ribbon visuals but collision is disabled (`collision_layer=0`) — **ACTIVE-only road physics**, analogous to terrain/water/biome.
- Clipping: only segments whose centerline lies inside the inflated rect are emitted; shared edges produce no duplication because each ribbon segment belongs to the chunks it actually crosses — but each chunk's mesh is clipped to its own rect so adjacent chunks agree within `SEAM_CONTINUITY_TOL_M 0.02` at the shared border (the centerline y at the border sample matches via shared world-space height sampling, and the ribbon's left/right edge x/z at `x = chunk edge` matches within `0.02` because both chunks sample the same world-space polyline at the same world param).
- Budget: each road chunk is bounded at **`<= 96 verts / <= 64 tris / 1 collider`** (typical single ribbon with 3-5 quads plus at most one bridge deck quad-strip). Multi-segment chunks (a junction where 2-3 polylines meet) may reach `<= 160 verts / <= 96 tris` but still `1` collider and must stay within `FRAME_BUDGET_MS 12` when combined with city/terrain/water/biome. The harness enumerates both typical and junction chunks; the global cap enforced by the builder is `road_vertices <= 160` and `road_triangles <= 96` and `road_colliders <= 1`.

Materialization `static materialize(parent: Node3D, manifest: Dictionary) -> Dictionary`:

- Creates `Road_X_Y` Node3D under chunk `Chunk_X_Y`, with `RoadMesh` (`MeshInstance3D` ribbon, one `ArrayMesh` combining all road+bridge strips for that chunk) and optional `RoadBody` (`StaticBody3D` + `CollisionShape3D`) under same parent. Mirrors `TerrainChunkBuilder.materialize` / `WaterChunkBuilder.materialize` / `BiomeChunkBuilder.materialize` signatures so `ChunkManager` can measure `road_mat_ms`. Returns stats `road_vertices, road_triangles, road_colliders, bridge_vertices, bridge_triangles, road_gen_ms, road_mat_ms, road_nodes, has_road, has_bridge`.
- Urban compatibility: road manifests are generated for all chunks, but inside `URBAN_INNER_M 350` flat the city street grid already owns ground — rural road ribbons there are suppressed (`has_road=false` if `origin.length() < URBAN_INNER_M` and no gate within `90 m`). Gates themselves (on the `URBAN_OUTER_M` ring) do emit a short `primary` stub into the city so the rural network visibly meets the city.
- Hydrology compatibility: over-water ribbon is `is_bridge` only; no dirt/road collider is placed with `water_body_at != &""` unless `is_bridge` true and water_id matches; bridge decks are the only road geometry that may sit at `water_level+0.35`.
