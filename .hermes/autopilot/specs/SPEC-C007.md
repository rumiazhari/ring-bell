## Player-facing objective

After `P4.3` the countryside is sheltered but spent after one loot: a player follows a grey `7.0 m` primary from a city gate, crosses the Vltava bridge `+0.35` over teal water `4a7a94` `bank 9` `floodplain 26`, arrives at a hamlet with `2-3` low-rise shells (plaster `ddd0c0` brick `b07a5a` timber `7a5a3a` roof `8a3a2a`/`5a5a5a`) set back `4 m`, opens an `E` door, sees a `0.95 m` partition doorway, shelf silhouette and a `FoodCrate Search shelves (Canned Food x2)` via `E` — and after taking it the hamlet is empty forever. There is no renewable water within a walk, no wild food off the road, no reason to return, and no choice between a road-side hamlet with a well and a field-edge forage thicket versus a village cluster with more crates. `P4.4` makes the hamlet a renewable homestead: deterministic village wells (stone-ring cylinder + water plane, `0.9 m` radius, `1.8 m` height) and wild forage patches (low bush `0.8x0.6x0.8` + mushroom proxy `0.4x0.3x0.4`) streamed with the same ACTIVE-only discipline as shells. A hamlet now yields `1` well, a village `1-2` wells; every rural settlement vicinity holds `2-5` forage patches tucked against field hedgerows and forest margins. Each well gives `Water Bottle x1` via `E` (prompt `Draw water (Water Bottle x1)` → `Well is dry — refills at dawn` after taking, refills deterministically at next game-day `04:00`); each forage gives `1` random `ItemDB` item weighted `canned_food 40 / water_bottle 25 / bandage 20 / antibiotics 15` but thematically reskinned as wild `mushroom/herbs` yielding the same registry entries (prompt `Forage bushes (Bandage x1)` → `Picked clean — regrows in 2 days`). The player can walk `600-900 m` along a rural corridor, spot a hamlet from a ridge, orient by the well beside its barn, forage along the hedgerow on the way, refill at the well, and decide to base at the hamlet with a reliable well versus the village with more crates but more exposure — a renewable arrival payoff that gives exploration a return, survival a countryside recovery loop without inventing a new item economy, and the road/settlement graph a reason to be followed twice. Future milestones will expand these anchors into field-parcel cultivation, orchard harvest, beekeeping, workbench cooking (`stove`), bed rest (`bed`) and full NPC habitation; this slice proves renewable siting, streaming and interactable honesty at the same budgeted discipline as interiors.

This is the next bounded milestone because the geographic logic stack plus thin interiors is landed — terrain `17x17 289/512 1 collider 9 active`, hydrology `Vltava CX 530-710 meander 72+18 width 38-50 9x9 81/128 9 active`, biome/geology `9x9 81/128 <=48 instances 9 active`, settlement `12-36 spaced 700/420/220+1.8r +4-8 gates`, road `MST+1-3 loops 96/64 typical 160/96 junction ACTIVE-only 9 active`, rural shells `1-6 per settlement cardinal yaw road-setback 4 spacing 8 aabb-gap 192/144 typical 320/240 400/300 with interiors 1 collider 9 active doors <=6` plus interiors `0-1 partition 1-3 furniture 0-3 crates 1/hamlet ItemDB 40/25/20/15 aabb-gap 0.9` — each proved deterministic, seam-continuous within `0.02` and ACTIVE-only. The hinterland has places, paths, walls, rooms and one-shot loot but no renewable water/food anchoring the settlement choice and no identifiable well/forage landmark visible from a hill. Without renewables every `rural_building_at(p)` cluster is depleted after one `E`, road-following has a single arrival, and future field cultivation, orchard, well-repair and village economy would be placed over an unproven renewability + persistence pattern. `P4.4` is the macro plan's "geographic logic precedes decoration" completion for Phase 4 renewable habitability — wells and forage must be established before field-parcel cultivation, orchard, workbench cooking or inhabited hamlets can be populated, and before underground refuge can compete as alternative shelter.

This slice is deliberately thin and additive: no new 64 m field-parcel tessellation (arable `arable_field` stays as biome vertex-color overlay + hedgerow MultiMesh, not a plowed mesh), no orchard row geometry, no full tenement/barn room graph beyond the single `P4.3` partition, no modular GLB assets, no rail mesh, no lake/reservoir basin formation, no terrain trench carve through the `350 m` historic basin, no underground cellar/cave, no toon shader rework, no NPC spawns inside rural shells beyond `FoodCrate` loot, no `stove` cooking or `bed` fatigue system in this slice (stove/bed remain visual proxies, their functionalization is deferred to the next homestead milestone). It adds deterministic renewable siting as pure plan data plus two low-cost per-chunk interactable families streamed ACTIVE-only.

## Gameplay value

Improves at least five enjoyment pillars in one thin slice while preserving budgets:

- **Survival pressure with recovery** — wells give renewable `Water Bottle` and forage renewable `canned_food/water_bottle/bandage/antibiotics` weighted `40/25/20/15` with deterministic regrow (`well 1` game-day `04:00`, forage `2` days). Failure in the city (thirst/hunger, infection) allows believable countryside recovery without inventing a new item economy; gathering is deliberate (`E` per harvest, prompt `Draw water`/`Forage bushes` updates to dry/picked then refreshes) and persists via deltas.
- **Exploration and discovery** — wells are a hill-visible landmark (`2.2 m` stone ring + dark water disc) beside a hamlet barn, forage bushes form a readable hedgerow line along the `arable_field` edge. A player can orient by "the hamlet well north of the ford" and return by the same road; patches are distinct across `256 m` landscape cells, not per-chunk speckles, with at least `2` distinct forage kinds and `2` distinct well clusters across `5`-seed matrix.
- **Agency and meaningful choice** — road vs hedgerow now has a payoff gradient: villages (`1-2` wells + `3-4` forage + more crates) reward `600-900 m` walks and bridge crossing with reliable water, hamlets offer quick single-well recovery with less exposure but limited forage. The player chooses which settlement kind to target based on thirst vs hunger vs risk, and where to base.
- **Coherent Czech identity** — Bohemian hamlet morphology plus farm-well (stone ring `8b7f6e`, dark water `2b3a4a`) plus hedgerow forage (pasture/orchard margin) makes the basin recognizably inhabited without claiming a 1:1 map; forage proxies use same vertex-color discipline as shells/wells.
- **Performance and stability foundation** — wells/forage reuse the measured streaming discipline: deterministic manifests, world-space center ownership, bounded verts/tris/colliders, ACTIVE-only physics for wells (forage has no collider, only `Interactable`), `t_rural_gen`/`t_rural_mat` telemetry within `FRAME_BUDGET_MS 12`, save exclusion of generated shells/interiors/crates/wells/forage (only `seed/version/discovery/deltas` plus wells/forage deltas), `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 2 (additive outside dense core).

Cosmetic polish accompanies but does not replace the playable system: wells are thin vertex-colored cylinder + water disc + wooden beam, forage are small `BoxMesh` proxies inside the same `ArrayMesh` batch plus `Area3D` interactable cylinders, not imported modular assets or shader rework; both use the existing `InteractableComponent` contract and `ItemDB` table.
