## Player-facing objective

After `P4.1` the world has readable roads and settlement anchors but the countryside is still empty at the destination: a player follows a paved `7.0 m` primary from a city gate, crosses the Vltava-like bridge `+0.35` over teal water `4a7a94` `bank 9 m` `floodplain 26 m`, and arrives at a hamlet clearing with hedgerow dressing — yet no shelter, no door to open, no landmark to return to. `P4.2` turns those anchors into explorable rural fabric: deterministic low-rise building shells (village houses, cottages, barns, farmsteads) clustered around each `settlement_anchors()` site, aligned to the nearby road or settlement center, each with a physical doorway that obeys the existing `Door` contract (closed blocks aperture center, open clears without RID exclusion, swung leaf remains collidable, destroyable). The player can walk `600-900 m` along a rural corridor, see a hamlet of 2-3 houses and a farmstead barn from a hill, open a door with `E`, step inside a `6x8`--`10x12` m shell, and take shelter — a usable landmark that gives exploration a payoff, survival a countryside alternative to the city, and the road graph a reason to exist. Future milestones will elaborate these shells into full tenement/barn/farmhouse programs with furniture, workstations and inhabitants; this slice proves the siting, streaming and door honesty.

This is the next bounded milestone because the geographic logic stack is landed and streamed: terrain `17x17 289/512 1 collider 9 active`, hydrology `Vltava CX 620+-90 meander 72+18 width 38-50 9x9 81/128 9 active`, biome/geology `9x9 81/128 <=48 instances 9 active`, settlement anchors `12-36 spaced 700/420/220+1.8r +4-8 gates`, road graph hierarchical `MST+1-3 loops hydrology-constrained` with streamed ribbon `96/64 typical 160/96 junction ACTIVE-only 9 active` — each proved deterministic, seam-continuous within `0.02` and ACTIVE-only. The hinterland has places and paths but no built fabric; without shells every `settlement_at(p)` answer is invisible, road-following has no arrival, and future village interiors, farm production, rail sidings and quarry outposts would be placed arbitrarily over empty fields. `P4.2` is the macro plan's "geographic logic precedes decoration" gate for Phase 4 settlement habitation — shells must be established before building programs (Phase 7), farm production or upper-city retrofit.

This slice is deliberately thin and additive: no parcel street grid inside villages, no village block/district subdivision, no interior room/furniture graph, no modular GLB assets, no rail mesh, no lake/reservoir basin formation, no terrain trench carve through the `350 m` historic basin, no underground, no swimming, no toon shader rework. It adds deterministic rural building siting as pure plan data plus a single low-cost per-chunk shell builder streamed with the same budget discipline as terrain/water/biome/road.

## Gameplay value

Improves at least four enjoyment pillars in one thin slice while preserving budgets:

- **Exploration and discovery** — hamlets/villages become readable hilltop landmarks. A player can orient by "the barn hamlet north of the ford" and return by the same road; rural shells are visible from `400-600 m` on clear ridges and distinct across `256 m` landscape cells (not per-chunk speckles).
- **Agency and meaningful choice** — road vs cross-country now has a shelter payoff. The road is faster and orients, but exposes; cutting across field/forest to a farmstead barn offers concealment and a door to bar. The river bridge puzzle gains an arrival.
- **Survival pressure with recovery** — each village/hamlet provides immediate shelter (physical walls + closable door that still blocks when closed) and a future scavenge anchor. Failure in the city (zombie pressure, stamina) allows believable countryside recovery without inventing a new system.
- **Coherent Czech identity** — Bohemian hamlet morphology (2-3 houses + barn, farmstead on fertile loess, village spaced `700 m` on gentle terrain outside floodplain) plus roadside hedgerow/field context makes the basin recognizably Czech without claiming a 1:1 map.
- **Performance and stability foundation** — shells reuse the measured streaming discipline: deterministic manifests, world-space shared footprint ownership, bounded verts/tris/colliders/instances, ACTIVE-only physics, `t_rural_gen`/`t_rural_mat` telemetry within `FRAME_BUDGET_MS 12`, save exclusion of generated shells, `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 2 (additive outside dense core).

Cosmetic polish accompanies but does not replace the playable system: shells are flat vertex-colored boxes with primitive door leaves via the existing `Door` class, not imported modular assets or shader rework.

## Scope

### 1. Deterministic rural building plan

Create `world/generation/rural_building_plan.gd` (or `settlement_building_plan.gd` — exact file name may be adjusted but the plan class must be named `RuralBuildingPlan` or `SettlementBuildingPlan` and the WorldPlan forwarding names below must exist) as a pure plan (no Node access, no unseeded randomness, no chunk-local state) and expose it through `world/generation/world_plan.gd` facade.

Required queries (exact names may be adjusted but semantics must exist and be used by the materializer and tests):

- `rural_buildings() -> Array[Dictionary]` — all rural building shells deterministically scattered over `[-8192,8192)` world. Each dict: `{id: String, kind: StringName, center: Vector2, footprint: Vector2, yaw: float, height: float, floors: int, settlement_id: String, settlement_kind: StringName, door_pos: Vector2, door_yaw: float, slope_deg: float, dist_to_water: float, dist_to_road: float, strata: StringName, tile: Vector2i, aabb: Rect2}`. `kind` vocab subset of `WorldConstants.RURAL_BUILDING_VOCAB` (at least `&"village_house"`, `&"cottage"`, `&"barn"`, `&"farmhouse"`, `&"stable"`; `&"shed"` may appear for isolated farm). `footprint` is `Vector2(6-8, 8-12)` deterministically (`village_house` `8-10x10-12`, `cottage/farmhouse` `7-9x8-11`, `barn/stable` `9-12x14-18` but this slice keeps barn footprint `8-10 x 10-14` to fit `64 m` chunk budgets; height `4.2 + floors*2.9` with `floors 1` for track/field buildings and `1-2` for village houses (hamlet houses `1` only this slice to keep stair graph out of scope)). `door_pos` is on the footprint edge midpoint (deterministic choice among 4 edges) facing the nearest road (`distance_to_road < 22`) or else the settlement center. `aabb` is world-space `Rect2` of the oriented footprint for culling/overlap (axis-aligned bounding rect is sufficient for this slice).
- `rural_buildings_in(rect: Rect2) -> Array[Dictionary]` — shells whose `aabb` intersects `rect` or whose `center` lies inside `rect.grow(32)`.
- `nearest_rural_building(p: Vector2) -> Dictionary` — closest shell to `p` (empty dict if world empty, which never happens for canonical seeds).
- `rural_building_at(p: Vector2) -> Dictionary` — shell whose `aabb` contains `p` and whose oriented footprint contains `p` within `0.5 m` inset (or whose `center` distance `< footprint.length()*0.4` and not separated by water/road trench); otherwise empty.
- `settlement_buildings(settlement_id: String) -> Array[Dictionary]` — buildings belonging to that settlement anchor.

Generation contract:

- Building siting uses `WorldSeed.sample_coherent` / `sample_coherent_signed` / `unit_float` with explicit **rural building domains** and `MACRO_CELL_M 1024` / `LANDSCAPE_CELL_M 256` spacing, seed-separated from terrain/hydro/geology/biome/settlement/road domains. No RNG sharing, no per-query mutable state, handles negative world coordinates via `floori`.
- Per-settlement candidate generation: for each `settlement` anchor `s` from `SettlementPlan.settlement_anchors()`, determine target building count by kind: `village` `4-6` (mixed `village_house` `2-3`, `barn 1`, `cottage 1-2`), `hamlet` `2-3` (`village_house/cottage 1-2`, `barn/stable 0-1`), `farmstead` `1-2` (`farmhouse 1`, `barn/stable 0-1`), `isolated_farm` `1` (`farmhouse` or `barn`). Count is deterministic via `unit_float("rural_building_count", [hash(s.id), k])`.
- For each building `k` in settlement `s`, place candidate center `c_k = s.center + polar_offset`, where `radius = lerp(0.28, 0.82, unit_float("rural_building_radius", [hash(s.id), k])) * s.radius` plus small angular jitter `unit_float("rural_building_angle", [hash(s.id), k])* TAU`, then snapped to respect `RURAL_BUILDING_ROAD_SETBACK 4.0` from any road centerline (`road_network.distance_to_road(c_k) >= width*0.5 + 4.0` unless `k==0` which may be road-facing) and `>= RURAL_BUILDING_SPACING_MIN 8.0` from any previously placed building in same settlement (greedy by `k` order) and `>= RURAL_BUILDING_SETTLEMENT_INNER_CLEARANCE 6.0` from settlement center. If a candidate violates, perturb deterministically via `unit_float("rural_building_nudge", [hash(s.id), k, attempt]) * 12` at up to `5` attempts; if still invalid the building is dropped (keeps spacing contract) rather than overlapping.
- Footprint and orientation: `footprint = Vector2(lerp(FP_MIN.x, FP_MAX.x, unit_float("rural_building_fp_x", [hash(s.id), k])), lerp(FP_MIN.y, FP_MAX.y, unit_float("rural_building_fp_y", [hash(s.id), k])))` per kind caps from `WorldConstants.RURAL_BUILDING_FOOTPRINT_*`. `yaw` is deterministic `0, PI/2, PI, -PI/2` aligned to nearest road tangent (if `dist_to_road < 36`) else `unit_float("rural_building_yaw", [hash(s.id), k])` quantized to those 4 cardinals to keep batching simple and avoid arbitrary angles that complicate collision.
- Gates and urban exclusion: no rural building whose `aabb` intersects `circle length < URBAN_INNER_M` (except a gate hamlet's single roadside barn may sit at `URBAN_INNER_M+40` `<= 1` per gate, documented rarely). No building where `hydrology.water_body_at(c_k) != &""` or `is_floodplain(c_k)` or `distance_to_water <= BANK_W + 2` or `terrain.terrain_class_at(c_k)==&"cliff"` or `slope >= BUILDABLE_MAX_SLOPE_DEG` (`22`) — for `village_house` require `slope < 14`.
- Deterministic and additive: does not mutate `TerrainPlan` heights, does not carve `CityPlan` blocks, `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 2. Determinism holds for any seed and any query order, including negative coords.

### 2. Rural chunk materialization

Create `world/streaming/rural_building_chunk_builder.gd` and integrate through `WorldPlan` + `ChunkManager`.

Manifest per 64 m chunk via `static build_manifest(world_plan: WorldPlan, coord: Vector2i) -> Dictionary`:

- Keys: `coord, origin, size, rural_buildings (Array[Dictionary] clipped), door_manifests (Array[Dictionary]), rural_vertices, rural_triangles, rural_colliders, rural_doors, has_rural, rural_gen_ms` plus `aabbs` and `colors`. Deterministic for `(seed, coord)`.
- Sampling: for the chunk rect `Rect2(origin, Vector2(64,64))`, query `rural_building_plan.rural_buildings_in(rect)` (already clipped via aabb). For each building whose footprint center lies inside the chunk rect (ownership by footprint center — same rule as city buildings — so streaming never duplicates or tears a building), generate batched box geometry: one `ArrayMesh` combining all rural boxes for that chunk (vertex-colored, `StandardMaterial3D vertex_color_use_as_albedo=true`, no texture import). Palette: plaster `Color("ddd0c0")`, brick `Color("b07a5a")`, timber `Color("7a5a3a")`, roof `Color("8a3a2a")`/`Color("5a5a5a")` deterministically per `kind` via `unit_float("rural_building_palette", [hash(id)])`. Each building box is axis-aligned after yaw quantization (cardinal) with `footprint.x/z` and `height` (ground at `terrain.height_at(center)`). Door aperture is left as a wall opening (no interior partitions in this slice); a `Door` leaf will be instanced at `door_pos` with `door_yaw` (see materialize). Indices per box `12 tris` (closed shell) but batched mesh may share vertices; budget measured as `rural_vertices`/`rural_triangles`.
- Collision budget: at most **one rural collider per chunk** (0 if no building). The single collider is one aggregated `ConcavePolygonShape3D` or `BoxShape3D` set under `StaticBody3D RuralBody` (`collision_layer 1`, `mask 0`, `backface_collision=true` for Concave). Door leaves retain their own `RigidBody3D` collision (per-door, but `rural_colliders` counts only the aggregated shell collider; door colliders are counted in `rural_doors` and `planned_colliders` separation as in CityPlan — spec requires rural shell collider ≤1, door leaves are additional physics bodies governed by Door contract and not counted toward the shell budget). Warm retains visuals but shell collision is disabled (`collision_layer=0`) — **ACTIVE-only rural physics**, analogous to city/terrain/water/biome/road.
- Clipping and seam: buildings belong to exactly one chunk by center; adjacent chunks agree because each building appears only in its owner chunk. Shared chunk borders carry no duplicated walls; the empty field between buildings is traversable terrain/biome.
- Doors: for each building in manifest, emit a door manifest entry `{id: "rural_door_%s_%d" % [building.id, 0], building_id: building.id, pos: door_pos, yaw: door_yaw, kind: &"rural_house"}`. The chunk builder does not create physics yet; materialize does. At most `6` doors per village chunk, `3` per hamlet/farmstead chunk (matches building counts).
- Urban compatibility: rural manifests generated for all chunks, but inside `URBAN_INNER_M 350` rural buildings are suppressed (`has_rural=false` if `origin.length() < URBAN_INNER_M` and not gate hamlet barn). Gates may host at most `1` roadside barn.

Materialization `static materialize(parent: Node3D, manifest: Dictionary) -> Dictionary`:

- Creates `Rural_X_Y` Node3D under chunk `Chunk_X_Y`, with `RuralMesh` (`MeshInstance3D` batched ArrayMesh) and optional `RuralBody` (`StaticBody3D` + `CollisionShape3D`) and per-building `Door` instances (from `world/buildings/door.gd`) parented under the same `Rural_X_Y` or under `Chunk_X_Y` as `RuralDoor_*` nodes, mirroring `CityPlan` door ownership but with `rural_` prefix to avoid duplicate IDs. Measures `rural_mat_ms`. Returns stats `rural_vertices, rural_triangles, rural_colliders, rural_doors, rural_buildings, rural_gen_ms, rural_mat_ms`.
- Hydrology/road compatibility: no rural building `aabb` may intersect water/floodplain or lie on a road ribbon (`distance_to_road < width*0.5+2.5` rejected at plan time); if a road edge was later rerouted near a building, the building stays and the road manifest's hydrology constraint already avoided water; no dirt/road collider and rural shell collider share the same `collision_layer 1` but do not overlap in world XZ.
